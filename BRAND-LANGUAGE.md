# NEWITT MEDIA brand language

NEWITT MEDIA is Ian Newitt’s personal creative passion project and hobby. Write about what Ian makes, explores and shares. Use first person where Ian is speaking and use NEWITT MEDIA when identifying the work.

Keep the three creative divisions, official logos and established imagery. The homepage heading is **Three worlds. One passion.** The official slogan is **FROM ABOVE. AFTER DARK. AND EVERYTHING IN BETWEEN.**

Write about drone flying, aerial cinematography, photography, filmmaking, original music, paranormal investigations and exploring new places. Invite people to watch, listen, explore, share thoughts and follow the creative journey.

Avoid implying a company, commercial service, client relationship, sales offer, revenue target or corporate growth plan. Check headings, contact copy, descriptions, metadata and share titles before building a preview. Preserve accurate legal and privacy copy, including references to third-party platforms and services.
