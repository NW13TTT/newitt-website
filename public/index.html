Meet Ian Newitt

I’m Ian Newitt, the person behind NEWITT MEDIA. I use drones and photography to show places from angles you don’t normally get to see. With Newitt’s Paranormal Adventures, I explore historic locations after dark and share what it’s like to be there. I also make original music, including Beyond The Skyline, to bring some of that atmosphere into my films.
