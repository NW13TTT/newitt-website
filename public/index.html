THE NEWITT MEDIA STORY

One brand. Three perspectives.

NEWITT Media is built around a simple idea: great stories can be found anywhere. Sometimes they are high above the landscape. Sometimes they are captured through a camera. Sometimes they are hidden inside places with a history that refuses to be forgotten.

01

Aerial Perspective

Drone photography gives landscapes, buildings, locations and events a completely different perspective. NEWITT Media focuses on cinematic aerial imagery that shows places from above and captures details people may never normally see.

02

Photography

Photography sits at the heart of NEWITT Media. Whether it is a landscape, a historic location, an unusual moment or a carefully composed image, the aim is always to create photography with atmosphere, character and a reason to stop and look.

03

The Unexplained

NEWITT's Paranormal Adventures explores historic and reportedly haunted locations. Investigations focus on documenting the experience, researching the history and presenting what happens through video, photography and storytelling.
