Behind the name

About NEWITT Media

NEWITT Media is an independent creative project bringing together aerial drone work, photography and paranormal adventures under one roof.

From views captured high above the ground to locations explored after dark, the aim is simple: capture experiences, tell the story and give people a different perspective.

Each side of NEWITT Media has its own identity, its own content and its own adventure, while remaining part of one larger brand.
