About NEWITT Media

One brand. Multiple perspectives.

Drone Photography

NEWITT Skyline Media uses aerial photography and video to capture locations from a perspective that traditional photography simply cannot provide.

From landscapes and buildings to events and places of interest, the aim is to create professional-looking footage that gives viewers a completely different view.

Paranormal Adventures

NEWITT's Paranormal Adventures documents investigations at locations with reported paranormal activity and fascinating histories.

The focus is on exploring locations, documenting what happens and creating entertaining content while respecting the history and character of every place visited.

Photography

Photography sits at the heart of NEWITT Media, connecting the aerial, creative and paranormal sides of the business.

Every image is about finding an interesting perspective and creating something worth remembering.

What NEWITT Media Stands For

NEWITT Media brings together creativity, technology, exploration and storytelling.

Whether it is an aerial view from above, an investigation after dark or a photograph captured at exactly the right moment, the goal is simple: create content people want to watch, share and remember.
