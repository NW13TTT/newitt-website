About NEWITT Media

One brand built around exploration, creativity and seeing the world from a different perspective.

Drone & Photography

NEWITT Media uses aerial technology and photography to capture places in ways that traditional photography cannot.

From sweeping aerial landscapes to detailed photographs on the ground, the aim is to create distinctive visual content that tells the story of a location.

Through NEWITT Skyline Media, drone footage becomes another way of exploring and documenting the world around us.

Paranormal Adventures

NEWITT's Paranormal Adventures explores historic, unusual and reportedly haunted locations.

Investigations are documented through video, photography and live content, allowing viewers to experience the locations alongside the team.

The focus is on the location, the history, the investigation and the experience.
