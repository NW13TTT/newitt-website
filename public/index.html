Why NEWITT Media?

NEWITT Media is an independent creative project bringing together aerial drone work, photography and paranormal adventures under one roof.

There is no giant production company hiding behind the curtain. Just cameras, drones, ideas, questionable amounts of equipment and a determination to make something worth watching.

Whether the camera is in the air, in a haunted building or simply pointed at something worth remembering, NEWITT Media is about creating and sharing experiences.
