The idea

Find the story. Test the evidence.

Haunted locations are full of stories. Our job is to explore them, document what happens and let people make up their own minds.

Sometimes the building creaks. Sometimes something genuinely odd happens. Either way, the investigation is about documenting the experience rather than deciding the answer beforehand.

No pretending every strange noise is a ghost. No declaring victory because the lights flickered once. We investigate, record and see where the evidence takes us.

Places with a story

The next investigation could be anywhere.

Historic buildings, former prisons, reported haunted locations and places where the atmosphere changes when the sun goes down.

NEWITT's Paranormal Adventures is about getting out there and experiencing these locations for ourselves.

From Gloucester Prison to famous haunted locations such as 30 East Drive, every place brings its own history, stories and unanswered questions.

Some nights bring evidence. Some nights bring absolutely nothing. And occasionally the only thing haunting the investigation is the lack of sleep.
