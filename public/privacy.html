Privacy Policy

Your information. Your choice.

This privacy policy explains how NEWITT Media handles personal information when you contact us or use this website.

We aim to keep things simple and only use information where there is a genuine reason to do so.

What information may we receive?

If you contact NEWITT Media, you may choose to provide information such as your name, email address and the details of your enquiry.

Our website may also receive limited technical information needed for the website to operate, such as basic browser or device information.

How do we use it?

Information you provide may be used to:

  • respond to your enquiry;
  • discuss or provide a requested service;
  • communicate with you about your enquiry; or
  • maintain and operate the website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

This website may contain links to or embedded content from services including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

When you use those services, their own privacy policies and terms may apply. NEWITT Media does not control how those companies process information.

Cookies

NEWITT Media aims to keep the use of cookies and tracking technologies to a minimum. Third-party embedded content may use cookies or similar technologies according to the policies of the service providing that content.

How long do we keep information?

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, or where we have a legitimate reason or legal requirement to keep it for longer.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights concerning your personal information. These can include asking for access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion where applicable.

If you have a question about information you have provided to NEWITT Media, please contact us.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of sending or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This website is not specifically directed at children and NEWITT Media does not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy when our website, services or the way we handle information changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact NEWITT Media

If you have a question about this privacy policy or about personal information you have provided, please contact NEWITT Media.
