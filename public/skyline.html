Gloucester Prison

A historic former prison site in Gloucester, explored by NEWITT Skyline Media from an aerial perspective.

Safle hen garchar hanesyddol yng Ngloucester, wedi'i archwilio gan NEWITT Skyline Media o safbwynt awyr.

HISTORICAL BACKGROUND CEFNDIR HANESYDDOL

A brief history Hanes byr

The site has a long history associated with Gloucester's former castle and prison buildings. The medieval castle was built during the 12th century.

Mae gan y safle hanes hir sy'n gysylltiedig â hen adeiladau castell a charchar Gloucester. Adeiladwyd y castell canoloesol yn ystod y 12fed ganrif.

A new gaol was constructed on the site between 1786 and 1791. The prison was subsequently enlarged, including significant works during the 19th century.

Adeiladwyd carchar newydd ar y safle rhwng 1786 a 1791. Ehangwyd y carchar wedyn, gan gynnwys gwaith sylweddol yn ystod y 19eg ganrif.

A substantial prison block was constructed between 1844 and 1850. The prison continued operating into the modern era before eventually closing in 2013.

Adeiladwyd bloc carchar sylweddol rhwng 1844 a 1850. Parhaodd y carchar i weithredu i'r cyfnod modern cyn cau yn 2013.

This section describes the documented history of the location. Any paranormal investigations, experiences or theories associated with the site are separate from the historical record.

Mae'r adran hon yn disgrifio hanes dogfennol y lleoliad. Mae unrhyw ymchwiliadau paranormal, profiadau neu ddamcaniaethau sy'n gysylltiedig â'r safle ar wahân i'r cofnod hanesyddol.
